plaintasks keyboard commands

Single-letter keys work while the task list has focus. Press question mark in the app for the same help; Escape closes it.
Each task row reads its name, then what it is (task, subtask, level 2 subtask, bucket, list, group, section, folder, note or unmarked line), then its status (for example "Call Sam. task. Open."). Lists are named by their title line or file name, never by their path. Press Enter on a task to hear its full details.


MOVING AROUND

Up arrow and Down arrow: previous or next item.
Home and End: first or last item.
Page Up and Page Down: move ten items.
Right arrow: expand a task to show its notes and subtasks, or move to its first subtask. Tasks with notes start collapsed.
Left arrow: collapse a task, or move to its parent.
Enter: open the actions menu for the item. It starts with the item's full details: status, dates, file and line, notes, lists, Jira, and which buckets apply and whether they are inherited from the parent task or the list. On a group, Enter expands or collapses it.
Escape: close any dialog, including this help.


VIEWS

1: all open tasks.
2: tasks by file.
3: tasks by group.
4: schedule.
5: waiting on me.
6: waiting on others.
7: important.
8: in progress.
9: completed.
0: triage, for reviewing unmarked lines. Lines that are already tasks are not listed.
Pressing a view key says the view name straight away, before the first item is read.
Minus: My list.
Equals: all lists.
Shift and g: Buckets, each bucket followed by its tasks, then No bucket: how many open and done tasks have no bucket, and the open ones.
Shift and t: My Day. Tasks you picked for today, earlier picks still open (spoken as carried over), overdue tasks and tasks due today, High priority first. Ongoing tasks, waiting on others, blocked tasks and tasks on not-actionable lists are left out. The status line gives the counts.
Shift and a: Next actions. Open tasks with no open subtasks, grouped by file, High priority first, then in progress. A task with subtasks shows its subtasks instead. Same exclusions as My Day.
Left bracket and right bracket: previous or next list.
v: jump to the view menu.
c or h: show or hide completed tasks.
Slash: jump to search. Escape in search clears it.
r: rescan the files now.
Question mark: open the keyboard help.


STATUS

Plus or x: mark done or not done. A done task gets done: and today's date.
Greater than or w: waiting on someone else.
Less than: waiting on me.
Percent or p: in progress.
Asterisk or shift and i: important.
i: read everything about the selected row (dates, status, priority, blockers, list, line, notes) without opening anything.
Shift and s, on a task: open its subtasks in a box. Arrows move, right opens, left closes, i reads details, Escape closes. It says so and opens nothing if there are none. In triage, shift and s still marks the rest of the file as notes.
Tilde or b: ongoing or major outcome, written as a tilde.
When plus, greater than, less than, percent or asterisk turn a status on, a box asks for an optional note, for example who you are waiting on. Enter with nothing typed sets the status without a note. Escape cancels. The letters x, w, p, shift and i, and b set the status straight away with no note box.


EDITING TASKS

Tab: make the task a subtask of the task above it, with its notes and subtasks.
Shift and Tab: move a subtask up one level.
d: set or clear the due date and the check-in date. A time works too, for example fri 2pm.
e: rename the task and edit its notes in one box: the task name, then one field per note line in the file. Dates, status and other tokens on the task line are kept. Enter saves, Escape cancels, u undoes. Enter on a task also offers this as the first button, and Enter on a note row opens the box at that note.
s or ampersand: add a note under the task, after its other notes in the file.
t: pick the task for today, or take it out of My Day. A pick still open tomorrow carries over. Written as today: and a date.
Shift and p: cycle the priority: Medium, High, Low, Medium. Written as p:high or p:low; Medium has no token. Syncs to the Jira Priority field.
Shift and b: choose the tasks this one is blocked by. A checkbox list opens with a filter field: Up and Down move, Space ticks, Enter saves. Written as after: and the blocker's id:. A task is blocked until its blockers are done. Syncs as Jira Blocks links.
y: choose the task's buckets. A checkbox list of buckets opens with the current ones ticked; inherited ones say inherited from parent or from list. Up and Down move, Space ticks or unticks, Enter saves. Moving the mouse over an item focuses it so VoiceOver reads it. Ticking exactly the inherited buckets removes the task's own. On a file row in By file, or a list in All lists, y sets the list's default buckets, written on the file's first line.
o: open the task's file in your text editor.
u: undo the last change.
Delete or Backspace: in a normal list, take the item off that list. Anywhere else, and in a list that keeps its tasks in a file, delete the task and its subtasks. The text stays in the file with a dollar sign in front. u undoes it.


ADDING TASKS

n: add a task. Type to search the tasks you have, pick one to add to a list, or choose Create new task.
m: create a new task straight away, without searching.
Shift and n: in a list, add a note that is not a task to the list's notes file.


LISTS

l: choose which lists the task is on. Up and Down move, Space ticks a list, Enter saves. Ticking a list that keeps its tasks in another file moves the task there, with its notes and subtasks.
Shift and l: add the task to the list you are viewing or used last, or take it off.
Option and n: make a new list.
Shift and m: manage lists: create, rename, delete, folders, notes files, Jira sync settings per list, and Not actionable (events, meetings, ideas), which keeps a list's tasks out of My Day and Next actions.
Shift and o: sort a list by manual order, due date, check-in date, date added, type or name.
g: pick up the item to reorder it, in a list with manual order. Then Up, Down, Home and End move it, and Enter or Escape drops it.
Option and Up arrow, Option and Down arrow: move the item up or down one place in a list with manual order.


JIRA

j: Jira for this task: link it to an issue, see its status, or create an issue.
Shift and j: sync with Jira. Shows every change in both directions for the lists that sync to Jira, grouped as conflicts, new issues, updates to Jira and updates to files. Tick what you want, then Apply. Nothing is sent or written before that.


TRIAGE (press 0)

a or at sign: mark the unmarked line as a to-do.
Plus, less than, greater than, percent and asterisk (or x, w, shift and i, p): make the line a to-do with that status.
s or ampersand: mark the line as a note of the task above it.
Shift and s: mark every remaining line in the file as a note.
l: mark the line as a to-do, then choose its lists.
Tab: make the line a subtask of the task above it.
n and Shift and n: next or previous file.
Shift and r: mark the whole file as reviewed.
f: show only files that are new or changed since you reviewed them.
Enter: show the lines around the current line.
