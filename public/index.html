Manage lists

Lists are personal and are kept outside your text files. Deleting a list never changes your files. A list can have a folder to group it, and a file: either a notes file for notes that are not tasks, such as meeting notes, or a file the list keeps its tasks in. A list that keeps its tasks in a file shows everything in that file, and moving a task onto it moves the lines in your files.

      Keyboard shortcuts

      Use Up and Down to move through the list. Single-letter keys work while the list has focus. Escape closes this help.

      Shortcuts
      KeyWhat it does
      Up, DownPrevious or next item
      Home, EndFirst or last item
      Page Up, Page DownMove ten items
      RightExpand subtasks, or move to the first subtask
      LeftCollapse subtasks, or move to the parent
      EnterOpen the actions menu for the item, which starts with its full details (or expand and collapse a group). Rows read the name, then the status.
      plus, xToggle done (a done task gets done: and today's date)
      plus, greater than, less than, percent, asteriskWhen these turn a status on, a box asks for an optional note, for example who you are waiting on. Enter with nothing typed sets the status without a note. The note is saved under the task and shows when it is expanded. The letters x, w, p, i and b set the status straight away
      greater than, wToggle waiting on someone else
      less thanToggle waiting on me
      percent, pToggle in progress
      asterisk, iToggle important
      b or tildeToggle ongoing or major outcome, written as a tilde
      TabMake the task a subtask of the task above it, with its notes and subtasks. On an unmarked line it also marks it as a to-do, and if other lines sit between them it moves the line to the end of that task's block
      Shift and TabMove a subtask up a level. A bullet subtask at its parent's indentation loses its bullet instead. Tasks right after it at the old level become its subtasks
      dSet or clear the due date and the check-in date. A time works too: fri 2pm
      tPick the task for today, or take it out of My Day. A pick that is still open tomorrow carries over until you take it out. Written on the line as today: and a date
      shift and pCycle the priority: medium (no token), high (p:high), low (p:low). My Day and Next actions list High first, then Medium, then Low. Sync with Jira sends it to the Jira Priority field
      shift and bBlocked by: tick the tasks that must be done first. The task you wait for gets an id: token (once) and this task gets an after: token. A blocked task stays out of My Day and Next actions and is read as Blocked in other views, until every task it waits for is done or deleted
      jJira: link, status, create
      shift and jSync with Jira: shows every change in both directions for the lists that sync to Jira, grouped as conflicts, new issues, updates to Jira and updates to files. Tick what to do, then Apply. Nothing is sent or written before that
      yLink the task to goals (ongoing tasks, marked with ~). A goal with jira:label on its line is a label goal: Sync with Jira tags its linked tasks with a label such as bucket-advisory-services instead of making the goal an issue
      oOpen the source file in your text editor
      uUndo the last change
      s or &On a task: add a note to it, below its other notes in the file. On an unmarked line in triage: mark it as a note by writing "& " in front of it in the file; it attaches to the task above. In a file, any line starting with & is a note of the nearest task above it. Shift and s marks every remaining line in the file as a note
      EnterMenu with every action, including converting a task to a note
      nAdd a task: type to search the tasks you have, pick one to add to a list, or choose Create new task. In triage, n goes to the next file
      Option and nMake a new list
      Delete or BackspaceIn a list: take the item off that list. In a list that keeps its tasks in a file, and in any other view, including triage: delete the task and its subtasks. The text stays in the file with a dollar sign at the start, and the app stops showing it. u undoes it
      Right arrow, Left arrowExpand or collapse a task to show its notes and subtasks. Tasks with notes start collapsed
      mCreate a new task straight away, without searching
      shift and nIn a list: add a note that is not a task, to the list's notes file. In triage: previous file
      lChoose which lists the task is on. With only My list, it adds or removes straight away. In triage, it marks the line as a to-do first, then opens the list picker: Up and Down move through the lists, Space ticks one, Enter saves and moves on to the next line. Ticking a list that keeps its tasks in another file moves the task there, with its notes and subtasks
      shift and lAdd the task to the list you are viewing or used last, or remove it
      left bracket, right bracketPrevious or next list
      shift and mManage lists: create, rename, delete, folders, notes files
      shift and oIn a list: sort by manual order, due, check-in, date added, type or name
      gIn a list with manual order: pick up the item to reorder. Then Up, Down, Home and End move it, Enter or Escape drops it. In a list that keeps its tasks in a file, this moves the lines in the file, among the tasks at the same level
      Option and Up, Option and DownIn a list with manual order: move the item up or down one place
      minusOpen My list
      equalsOpen All lists
      shift and gOpen Goals: each goal with its linked tasks
      shift and tOpen My Day: tasks you picked for today (and earlier picks still open), overdue tasks and tasks due today, High priority first. Goals, tasks waiting on others, blocked tasks and tasks on not-actionable lists are left out
      shift and aOpen Next actions: open tasks with no open subtasks, grouped by file, High priority first, then in progress. A task with subtasks shows its subtasks instead. Same exclusions as My Day
      a or @Triage: mark the unmarked line as a to-do. The symbol keys also work on unmarked lines
      fTriage: show only files not yet reviewed or changed since review
      shift and rTriage: mark the whole file as reviewed
      1 to 9, 0Switch view: 1 all open, 2 by file, 3 by group, 4 schedule, 5 waiting on me, 6 waiting on others, 7 important, 8 in progress, 9 completed, 0 triage
      vJump to the view menu
      c or hShow or hide completed tasks
      slashJump to search
      rRescan the files now
